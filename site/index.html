      Methodology

      A fixed panel of companies (list and changes in the repository) is read every Tuesday from the official public job board APIs of Greenhouse and Lever and labelled with the ISO week that just ended. Technologies are matched with a fixed list of 70 word-boundary patterns over job titles and descriptions; a job can mention several. "New" means a job ID not present in the previous week's snapshot. Only counts are published, never job texts. Trucking data: new USDOT numbers added Monday–Sunday in the FMCSA Company Census File, without any personal contact data. Code: github.com/cleanyield-data/clean-yield-data.
